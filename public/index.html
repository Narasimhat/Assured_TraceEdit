METHOD & INTERPRETATION
Transparent methods.
Careful conclusions.
Bounded mixture inference
Independent nonnegative least-squares fitting of wild-type, bounded indel, dual-cut excision, and specified substitution proposals. An upstream anchor establishes orientation and a constant read offset. This is not the ICE or DECODR implementation.
Continuous chromatograms
Instrument-analyzed DATA9–12 channels, mapped by called peak positions. No smoothing, baseline subtraction, or removal of secondary peaks. A single amplitude divisor is shared by all four dyes in each window; heights cannot be compared across rows.
Research prototype
SNP signals are not calibrated allele fractions. Mixed traces cannot establish phase or genotype. Frameshift estimates do not prove loss of function. High R² does not validate the biological model. Confirm findings with independent evidence.
