Peaksplit

Splits the mixed Sanger peaks from an edited cell pool into the alleles that produced them: indels for knockouts, donor-templated changes for SNP knock-ins. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

New project clears reads, designs and results together. Choose files adds to the current project. Catalog imports and example data start a separate project.

For two-guide excisions such as MYD88, use TraceEdit with both guides. Peaksplit models one guide and deletions up to 60 bp.

    How it works: the edited read is aligned to the control upstream of the cut, then the per-position base signal after the cut is fitted (non-negative least squares) as a mix of wild type, every deletion and insertion near the cut, and the donor-derived allele, using the control's own peak shapes as the template. Knockout score is the share of frameshift or ≥ 21 bp indels; knock-in score is the share of the donor allele. Treat results with R² below 0.8 with caution and confirm critical clones by NGS.
